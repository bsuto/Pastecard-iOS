Pastecard Help

About

Pastecard is a small, single page for notes. You can add to, edit, or completely erase it however you like. It’s like the digital version of writing on the back of your hand. Try it for:

  • Shopping lists
  • To-dos and reminders
  • Navigation directions
  • Links to view later
  • Contact information
  • and more!

Using Pastecard

  • Click on the card to make it editable, and you can make changes or copy text to your clipboard.
  • Once you’re done editing, click or tap “Save” to save your changes permanently, or “Cancel” to discard your changes. Either button will return you to the card view.
  • If you use Pastecard when your Mac has no internet connection, Pastecard will attempt to display the last-saved version of your text in an offline read-only mode.
  • Under read-only mode, you won’t be able to make any edits until you’re back online.

Mac App

There are two general controls in Settings that affect the Pastecard window.

  • Turn on “Floating Window” to have the Pastecard window float above other windows. This is helpful if you want to use Pastecard like a widget.
  • Adjust the font size by dragging the slider.

By default, Pastecard only saves your card text to your Mac and does not sync it to the cloud.

  • To sign into an existing Pastecard account or create a new Pastecard account, go to Settings and click “Sign In or Sign Up”
  • Enter your Pastecard ID into the field at the top and click “Go” to sign in. Your changes will then be synced to the cloud.
  • Click “Create a Pastecard” to sign up for an account. You will then be signed in and your changes will be synced to the cloud.
  • To sign out of your account, go to Settings and click “Sign Out”
  • You can go back to only saving your text on your Mac by clicking “Use Without an Account”

Pastecard includes a browser extension to help you save links from Safari.

  • Enable it from the Extensions tab in Safari’s Settings window. More instructions from Apple
  • When you click the extension icon or use a keyboard shortcut, any selected text in the current browser tab will be added to the end of your Pastecard.
  • If there is no text selected, the current tab’s URL will be added to the end of your Pastecard.
  • No keyboard shortcut is set by default. Command-Shift-P is recommended.
  • There is a bookmarklet that provides the same functionality for other browsers.

Security

  • Pastecard does not use passwords: if someone knows your Pastecard URL, they have the same access you do. Please don’t use Pastecard for any sensitive information.
  • If you’re concerned about privacy, we recommend choosing an ID that you don’t use anywhere else.
  • Only the current version of your Pastecard is saved. There is no way to undo or recover old text.

  • The only data Pastecard explicitly collects from you is your ID and your card text. We never share this with anybody.
