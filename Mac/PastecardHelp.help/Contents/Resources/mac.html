Using the Mac App

Accounts and Cloud Sync

By default, Pastecard only saves your card text to your Mac and does not sync it to the cloud.

  • To sign into an existing Pastecard account or create a new Pastecard account, go to Settings and click "Sign In or Sign Up"
  • Enter your Pastecard ID into the field at the top and click "Go" to sign in. Your changes will then be synced to the cloud.
  • Click "Create a Pastecard" to sign up for an account. You will then be signed in and your changes will be synced to the cloud.
  • To sign out of your account, go to Settings and click "Sign Out"
  • You can go back to only saving your text on your Mac by clicking "Use Without an Account"

App Settings

There are two controls in Settings that affect the Pastecard window.

  • Turn on "Floating Window" to have the Pastecard window float above other windows. This is helpful if you want to use Pastecard like a widget.
  • Adjust the font size by dragging the slider.

Safari Extension

Pastecard includes a browser extension to help you save links from Safari.

  • Enable it from the Extensions tab in Safari's Settings window. More instructions from Apple
  • When you click the extension icon or use the keyboard shortcut, any selected text in the current browser tab will be added to the end of your Pastecard.
  • If there is no text selected, the current tab's URL will be added to the end of your Pastecard.
