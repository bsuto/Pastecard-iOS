Pastecard Basics

Pastecard is a small, single page for notes. You can add to, edit, or completely erase it however you like. It's like the digital version of writing on the back of your hand. Try it for:

  • Shopping lists
  • To-dos and reminders
  • Navigation directions
  • Links to view later
  • Contact information
  • and more!

Using Pastecard

  • Open the Pastecard app or go to your Pastecard URL to see your saved text in a readable card view.
  • Click on the card to make it editable, and you can make changes or copy text to your clipboard.
  • Once you're done editing, click "Save" to save your changes permanently, or "Cancel" to discard your changes. Either button will return you to the card view.
  • If your device has no internet connection, Pastecard will attempt to display the last-saved version of your text in an offline read-only mode.
