# Pastecard for iOS and macOS

## iOS

* Home screen widgets that show your card text
* Share extensions to append the current URL or selected text
* Available and extensible through Siri and Shortcuts
  * "Hey Siri, what's on my Pastecard?"
  * "Hey Siri, add something to my Pastecard."
* __Swipe up__ on the card to force a refresh, share the card text to another app, or sign in/out/up
* iOS 17.6 or newer required

## macOS

* Safari extension to append the current URL or selected text
  * No keyboard shortcut assigned by default, but Command-Shift-P is nice
* Setting for the Pastecard window to float (and act as a widget)
* Menu items to force a refresh, or share the card text to another app
* Sign in/out/up in Settings
* macOS 14.6 or newer required

## on both

* Use the app exclusively on your device with no account at all
* All the warm feelings of a native SwiftUI app
